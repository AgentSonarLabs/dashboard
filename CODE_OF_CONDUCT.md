# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion and review.
- Focus feedback on the work and explain disagreements clearly.
- Respect privacy; do not share another person's personal information without consent.
- Accept correction gracefully and follow maintainer direction in project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository hosting platform or to a maintainer. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports confidentially where practical and may issue a warning, restrict participation, or remove someone from project spaces. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
